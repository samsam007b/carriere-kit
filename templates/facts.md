# Facts

> The single source of truth for every number, title, and claim that goes on a CV, in an email,
> or in an interview. **Absolute rule: no figure leaves this file without being entered here
> first, and nothing enters here without being verifiable.**
>
> A CV, an email, or an interview answer that cites a figure not in this table gets corrected,
> not the other way around.

## Confidence levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Verified against a primary source you can point to (a contract, an export, an official record) |
| B | Verified but from a secondary source, or a reasonable calculation from A-level facts |
| C | Stated from memory, not yet verified; usable in a draft, never in a sent document |
