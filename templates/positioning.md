## 1. What is your signal?

Signaling theory (Spence, 1973): in a market with asymmetric information, a generic signal
shared by every other candidate doesn't distinguish anyone. What distinguishes a profile is a
signal that's **costly to fake**.

{{Your costly signals, listed. Shipped projects, checkable figures, results traceable by a
third party. A degree supports institutional credibility, never leads.}}

Practical consequence: {{every CV and pitch opens on the evidence}}.

## 2. What capital are you building?

Career Capital Theory (Newport): a sense of mission emerges from accumulating rare skills, it
isn't decided in advance.

{{The combination you're building. Is it rare? Relative to what exact reference population?
A skill is only rare relative to a defined group; name it.}}

{{Is this an "auction" market (several buyers value different kinds of capital differently) or

strategy: in the first case, keeping several leads open is rational.}}
