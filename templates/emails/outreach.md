# Email templates (EN)

> Use with [`method/02-outreach-email.md`](../../method/02-outreach-email.md). These are
> skeletons, not fill-in-the-blank forms: everything inside braces must be **specific**,
> otherwise the email is no better than a generic template.

**Notes**

- Salutation: `Dear {{First name}},` in formal contexts (law, finance, EU institutions,
  consulting partners), `Hi {{First name}},` in tech, startups and most UK/US business
  contexts. Never `Dear Sir or Madam` when a name is available. Never `To whom it may concern`.
- Sign-off: `Kind regards` (UK, EU), `Best regards` (neutral, works everywhere), `Best` (US,
  informal). Avoid `Yours faithfully` and `Yours sincerely` unless the register is deliberately
  formal.
- No em dash, no en dash, anywhere. Use a comma, a period, a colon or parentheses instead. This
  matters more in English than in most languages: generic English LLM output is saturated with
  the em dash, and it's the single most recognisable tell.
- Other tics to strip: *I hope this email finds you well*, *reach out*, *leverage*,
  *passionate about*, *excited about the opportunity*, *I would love to*, *delve*, *tapestry*,
  *in today's fast-paced world*, *not only X but also Y*. Full list in
  [`method/02-outreach-email.md`](../../method/02-outreach-email.md), PATTE-OUT-06.
- Word ceiling: 150 words.

## 1. Speculative application, no open role

```
Subject: Speculative application, {{field in 3 or 4 words}}

Dear {{First name}},

{{One sentence: who you are, with your most concrete proof. Degree as
support, never as the opening.}}

{{One or two sentences: the specific thing you observed on their side, and
what you bring to it. A factual observation, never a critique of their work.}}

CV attached.

Would you be open to a conversation about this?

Kind regards,
{{First name Last name}}
```

**The trap**: the second sentence. If it could be sent as is to another organisation, the email
is generic. It has to contain a fact that exists only there.

---

## 2. Specific open role

```
Subject: {{Exact job title}}

Dear {{First name}},

I am applying for the {{exact job title}} role. {{One sentence on the fit
between the role and your profile, with one quantified element.}}

{{One sentence: the part of your profile that answers the most demanding
requirement in the posting.}}

CV attached.

I am happy to discuss this further.

Kind regards,
{{First name Last name}}
```

Definite article: "**the** role", never "a role". See PATTE-OUT-03 in
[`method/02-outreach-email.md`](../../method/02-outreach-email.md).

---

## 3. Internship or traineeship

```
Subject: Application, {{title}}, {{period}}

Dear {{First name}},

{{Current or completed studies, plus what you built alongside, quantified.}}

{{The link to their activity: a case, a piece of news, a practice area.}}

CV attached. Available from {{date}}.

I am happy to discuss this further.

Kind regards,
{{First name Last name}}
```

---

## 4. Follow-up after silence

Four lines maximum.

```
Subject: Re: {{original subject}}

Dear {{First name}},

I am following up on my message of {{date}}.

{{One new element: something that moved on your side, or a development on
theirs. With nothing new, a follow-up is just a repetition.}}

Still glad to discuss it whenever it suits you.

Kind regards,
{{First name Last name}}
```

Never: complain about the silence, re-explain your profile, add another attachment, switch
arguments.

---

## 5. Follow-up after an out of office

Send 3 to 5 working days **after** their return date, never the same day.

```
Subject: Re: {{original subject}}

Dear {{First name}},

I sent the message below shortly before you went away, so I am bringing it
back up now that you are back.

{{One sentence recalling the substance.}}

Kind regards,
{{First name Last name}}
```

---

## 6. After an interview or a call

Within 24 hours. This is not a follow-up, it carries substance.

```
Subject: Thank you for the conversation on {{topic}}

Dear {{First name}},

Thank you for your time {{today / yesterday}}.

{{One substantive element: a fuller answer to a question you were asked
during the conversation, or a specific point discussed where you add
something. That is what separates this from a plain thank-you note.}}

{{Next step as they stated it, restated in your words.}}

Kind regards,
{{First name Last name}}
```

---

## 7. Flagging an application filed through a portal

When the formal application went through the portal and you want a human to see it.

```
Subject: Application submitted: {{job title}}

Dear {{First name}},

I submitted my application for the {{job title}} role on {{date}}
{{reference if there is one}}.

{{One sentence, the strongest part of your profile for this role.}}

I am available if a conversation would be useful.

Kind regards,
{{First name Last name}}
```

Deliberately short: the full application already sits in their system, this email only makes
it visible.

---

## Checklist before sending

- [ ] Objective settled before writing
- [ ] Under 150 words
- [ ] Named salutation, correct register (`Dear` vs `Hi`)
- [ ] Specific hook, one that would not work anywhere else
- [ ] "CV attached." present, and the attachment actually is
- [ ] Open CTA, no fixed duration, no promised demo
- [ ] Sign-off and signature
- [ ] Zero em dash, zero tic from the PATTE-OUT-06 list
- [ ] Every figure checked against `workspace/facts.md`
- [ ] Correct sending address, consistent with the one on the CV
- [ ] Language verified, not assumed
- [ ] **Explicit confirmation obtained in the current turn**
