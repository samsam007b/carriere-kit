## Objective

A junior role in institutional communications, on the public affairs side. No posted opening:
speculative application, with an immediate objective of **a conversation**, not a hire. The
objective sets the register and the CTA of the email (see
[`method/02-outreach-email.md`](../../method/02-outreach-email.md), Step 0).

## Angle

In February they published a 40-page memorandum aimed at the incoming government, whose entire
press footprint was a single release with no visual or short-form version. That gap between
the substance produced and how little it circulated is the specific fact that justifies
writing to this person.

Phrased as an observation, never a critique of their work: "a 40-page memorandum that only got
a single press release" is an observation. "Your communications didn't do the document
justice" is a critique, and it closes the door.

**What I don't say**: that I could "overhaul their communications strategy." A junior
candidate promising an overhaul from the outside signals they haven't understood the role.

## Language

**English** (in the real market this example loosely draws on, French or Dutch would be
weighed the same way). Signals, most to least reliable:

1. The contact's LinkedIn profile and recent posts are in English.
2. The organisation's site is available in English and a second language; the careers page
   exists in both.
3. The memorandum cited is published in English, with a translated version.

Signal 1 settles it. If the contact posts in a different language than the organisation's
default site language, the contact's own language wins.

## Draft to sent

Logged right after sending, comparing against the message in Sent.

| Draft | Sent | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| "your memorandum, particularly strong on substance" | "your February memorandum" | Cut the flattery, keep the dated reference |
| "a gap I would be delighted to help close" | "a gap I know how to close" | Drop the polite conditional; it weakens the one sentence that claims a skill |
| "Would you have 20 minutes to spare?" | "Would you be open to a conversation about this?" | Drop the fixed duration; the CTA opens, it doesn't book a slot |
